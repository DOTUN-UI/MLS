Work Life

Life on
matchday

Flexible event schedules, training that sets you up to succeed, and a crew that moves together when the stadium fills.

Flexible by design

Most roles are event-based or part-time — built around home matches and special events. You pick availability; partners schedule shifts that fit.

Training that sticks

Before your first assignment, you'll complete orientation, guest service, safety, and accessibility modules so you show up ready.

Clear expectations

Once hired, you’ll get your next match, shift time, reporting gate, uniform, supervisor, and check-in steps — so you arrive confident.

Growth paths

Start as seasonal crew, grow into leads and supervisors, or move across departments as you gain experience across the venue.
